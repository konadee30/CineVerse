CINEVERSE - ADDING A MOVIE VIDEO

1. Open the Movie website/videos folder.
2. Copy your authorized MP4 video into that folder.
3. Use the exact filename shown in the matching watch-*.html page.
   Example: videos/frozen.mp4
4. If your video has a different filename, open its watch page and edit the <source src="..."> value.
5. Open the movie details page and click Watch Now. It opens a separate watch page.
6. For public viewing, upload the website AND video files to a web host that supports video hosting.

Each movie has its own watch page named watch-MOVIE-SLUG.html.
No JavaScript is used; the player is the built-in HTML5 <video controls> element.
Only upload movies you own or are licensed/authorized to distribute.
