HOW TO ADD MOVIES TO CINEVERSE

1. Open the Movie website/videos folder.
2. Copy your authorized .mp4 file into that folder.
3. Rename it to match the movie HTML page. For example, frozen.html uses videos/frozen.mp4.
4. Open that movie page in a browser. Scroll to the Watch section or click Watch Now.
5. Press Play in the built-in HTML5 video player.

The website has been prepared with a video player on every individual movie detail page.
To add a different movie later, create its details page, copy the video player section, and change the MP4 source path.

IMPORTANT: The MP4 files must be uploaded to the same hosting service as the website for public streaming.
Do not distribute copyrighted films unless you own them or have permission.

Technologies: HTML, CSS and Bootstrap. No JavaScript is used for the video player.
